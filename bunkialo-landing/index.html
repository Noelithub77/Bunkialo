02 The bunk math

Every bunk, counted.

Each course allows two bunks per credit, plus one. Bunkialo reads every absence from the attendance portal, leaves out the ones you mark as duty leave, and shows what's left. Tap the number to switch to the other view: how far you are above the 80% line right now.

3 credits · 7 bunks a semester

Every session, in one place

Each course card shows your percentage, bunks left and every session in a calendar. If the portal marked you absent by mistake, or you were away on duty leave, mark it and the numbers update.

03 One app

Every campus portal, together.

Deadlines live on Moodle, attendance on its own portal, the calendar in a PDF and the mess menu on a notice board. Bunkialo signs in once, keeps a copy for when you're offline, and turns it all into the few things you need today.

04 Your day

Everything else, sorted.

What's next, at a glance

The class you're in, the one after it, the next meal and every upcoming deadline, on one screen. Reminders arrive before things are due.

A timetable that writes itself

Your week is built from attendance records, so it matches the classes that actually happen. Two-hour slots become labs on their own, and any slot can be fixed by hand.

What's for lunch?

The mess menu with the next meal first, and the whole day laid out as a timeline.

Find any professor

Search the faculty directory by name, course or research area. It forgives typos, so “machine lerning” still finds the right people.

Plan the semester

Work out your SGPA and CGPA from credits and grades. Keep the academic calendar close, with exams, holidays and events you can add to your own calendar.

05 WiFix

Campus Wi‑Fi that signs itself in.

On campus Wi‑Fi nothing loads until you log in on a portal page, and it keeps asking again. WiFix notices that page, signs in with your LMS username and password, and only says you're online once the internet actually answers.

In the Android app (off until you turn it on), the CLI as wifix, and the Omarchy plugin.

07 For your agent

Let your agent handle campus.

Add the Bunkialo skill and CLI, and your coding agent can work every IIIT Kottayam portal for you: read attendance, find deadlines, download files, submit assignments and log in to Wi‑Fi.

your agentbunkialo skill

Check my attendance and find me the best way to compensate with duty leaves based on nearby events

  • bunkialo attendance sessions IHS311

HRM is your only course under 80%, at 8 of 11. Attend the next three classes and claim duty leave for the hackathon on 24 October, which clashes with the fourth. That brings you to 12 of 15: exactly 80%.

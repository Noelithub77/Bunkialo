# Bunkialo v1.5.1

- Smoother tab swipes, with shorter gestures accepted and pull-to-refresh kept separate from horizontal navigation.
- Course feedback under More and Settings. Submit without waiting for the full scan; completed forms are skipped, transient failures retry up to four times, and final status is verified with the LMS. Participation and ratings remain your choice.
- Full-day duty leave removes absences from attendance calculations while preserving present sessions. Attendance rows show status separately from manual entries.
- More sits at the top of the floating menu, with Course feedback and Academic calendar inside.
- Android notifications return to the full-height inbox.
- Pastel timetable and mess widgets have picker previews and size-aware details. Browse the full week with previous/next controls and a page count. Larger widgets show more menu details and following items; native layout and incremental-build fixes keep the cards current.

The Android runtime is `1.5.1-sdk58-widgets-pdf-v1`. Existing installations retain their own compatible update runtimes.

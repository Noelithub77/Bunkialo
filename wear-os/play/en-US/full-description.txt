Bunkialo is an attendance and assignment tracking app built for IIIT Kottayam students. It integrates with the institute’s official LMS and helps you with up-to-date attendance records, assignment deadlines, and smart reminders.

With Bunkialo, you no longer need to manually check Moodle every day. The app automatically syncs your data and presents it in a clean, easy-to-use interface designed for students.

Key Features

1) Attendance Tracking:-
Automatically syncs attendance data from IIIT Kottayam Moodle
View detailed attendance records for all enrolled courses
Track present, absent, late, and excused sessions
Calculate attendance percentages with course-wise breakdowns
Calendar-style view to visualize attendance history

2) Assignment Management
Timeline view of all upcoming assignments and deadlines
Automatic detection of overdue assignments
Direct links to Moodle assignment pages
Smart reminders for approaching deadlines

3) Smart Notifications
Background data refresh to keep information up to date
Customizable reminder notifications before deadlines
Attendance threshold alerts
Offline access using cached data

4)Modern Interface
Clean and minimal design for easy navigation
Dark mode and light mode support
Fast and responsive performance
Secure credential storage using device-level encryption

How It Works

1. Secure Login
   Log in using your IIIT Kottayam Moodle credentials

2. Auto Sync
   Attendance and assignment data are fetched automatically

3. Track and Monitor
   View attendance statistics and assignment timelines in one place

4. Stay Notified
   Receive timely reminders for deadlines and attendance limits

Privacy and Security

Your lms credentials are stored securely on your device
No data is shared with third parties

Wear OS companion

View your class timetable and mess menu on your watch, including offline access to the saved schedule. Sync your selected timetable from the Bunkialo Android app.

Add the Bunkialo tile to see your current or next class and its time. Tap the tile to open the watch app.

Add the Bunkialo watch face complication to see a class abbreviation at a glance. Tap the complication to open the corresponding timetable entry.
